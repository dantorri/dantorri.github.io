# Design

An engineer's project journal: warm paper, green-black ink, copper links, large serif titles, and plainly captioned original artifacts. Replace blue gradients and repeated rounded cards.

## Tokens
- Paper #f5f3ed; white #fffefa; ink #203b35; secondary ink #58645d.
- Accent #a13e24; rule #c9cdc2; dark surface #203b35.
- Display: self-hosted Instrument Serif. Text: self-hosted DM Sans.
- Maximum canvas 1368px; reading width 72ch. Fluid gutters 22–64px.

## Composition
Introduce Daniel beside an actual CAD assembly with selectable views. Follow with asymmetric project spreads, a compact index of smaller projects, professional background, and contact. Titles name projects or questions without paired eyebrow labels.

Case studies use a large title, concise summary, named scope and contribution, captioned media, and a next-project link. Preserve source-order readability on mobile.

## Interaction and accessibility
CAD buttons swap assembly views and update accessible state. Gait animations play only on request, with pause. Native details disclose technical notes. Visible focus, reduced-motion handling, no scroll reveals. Main content and links work without JavaScript.
